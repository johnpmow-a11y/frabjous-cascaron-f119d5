MediaPipe Tasks Vision 1.2.3

Pose and hand landmarks used for optional local body tracking are processed in the browser on the device. The MediaPipe privacy notice states that camera input is not sent to Google servers for this processing, but the library may send performance and usage metrics. The application asks the caller before enabling camera-based tracking.
